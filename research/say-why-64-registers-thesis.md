## The thesis

Say Why is not a form to fill. When someone clicks it, it should open into
**sixty-four registers**, modes of speech and presence, surfaced in film and
production.

The sixty-four are not invented. They are the structure already sitting in
`gene-keys-data`: sixty-four positions, each carrying three frequencies (Shadow,
Gift, Siddhi), six lines, programming partners, codon rings. The I Ching count.
The Gene Keys count. The count of the genetic substrate. Say Why borrows the
structure, not Rudd's text, the same wall the dataset already keeps.

Each register is a way of saying the thing. Placed on the **torus of
consciousness** (the four axes the Alchemy Diagnostic already names: intake,
transformation, expression, return flow), the sixty-four turn through cycles and
spirals. A person clicks Say Why, lands in a register, and films from there.
Economic material realities sit on that same spectrum, not outside it.

So the product seed is concrete:

- **Substrate:** the 64 positions in `gene-keys-data` (structure only, content referenced by id).
- **Surface:** Say Why opens to a register, not a blank field.
- **Medium:** film and production. The register is something you speak and shoot, not something you score.
- **Geometry:** the torus. Intake, transformation, expression, return flow. Cycles and spirals.
- **Posture:** mischief and presence (see consolidation doc, section 6). The seat, posture, breath.

This is the soul under the consolidation work in
`research/profile-consolidation-analysis.md`. The consolidation gives the one
public profile its plumbing. This gives it its reason.
