# Evaluation-Forward Hub: a framing to react to
*Working Document | Created: 2026-06-17*
*Purpose: Give the strategic question from the 2026-06-17 sweep something
concrete to push against. If evaluation is the real near-term business, here is
how the consolidated hub reads. Not a decision. A draft to argue with.*

---

## The question this answers

The 2026-06-17 All Systems Go sweep surfaced a gap between story and money. The
pipeline that is actually moving is high-ticket evaluation, the McEvoy ($45–75K),
Grossman ($35–60K), and RIPCA ($25K) threads. The Founder Story film tier
($1,500–2,500) has zero active deals. So the question:

> Is RP's near-term business the evaluation lane, with film as the
> differentiator, rather than film with evaluation as a credential?

The consolidation doc locked a practice-first frame that leads with film and
facilitation. If the answer above is yes, the hub should lead with evaluation.
This document drafts that inversion so the choice is concrete.

## Two framings, side by side

| | **Film-forward (current)** | **Evaluation-forward (this draft)** |
|---|---|---|
| Hero line | Facilitation and film for people in transition | Evaluation that captures what programs actually cost and change |
| First ask | Founder Story conversation | A 6-month learning partnership |
| Lead price | $1,500–2,500 | $25K–75K |
| Film's role | The product | The differentiator: evaluation you can feel, not just count |
| Proof on hand | One GDC video artifact | The H.R.1 cross-state tracker, the RIPCA proposal, the MPH |
| Who it converts | Individuals in transition | Associations, agencies, foundations with budget lines |

## Draft hero copy (evaluation-forward)

Plain register, RP voice rules (no em-dashes, concrete nouns, named things):

> **Isaac Rubinstein, Rubinstein Productions**
> Developmental, narrative-based evaluation for public health and
> mission-driven programs. I document what implementation actually costs your
> people, in a form your board and your funders can both use. The work is
> interview-grounded, and when it serves the story, it ships as film.

Subtitle candidates still open (from section 6 of the consolidation doc):
Information Alchemy, the chemistry of information. Both read more poetic than an
agency officer expects. Hold them for the thinker layer, not the hero line.

## What this does to the maps

The hub-and-spoke target in the consolidation doc still holds. Only the ordering
of the spokes changes:

1. **Evaluation lane moves to the front.** It carries the budget and the warm
   threads. The `three-type-evaluation` methodology becomes a primary page, not
   a folded-in afterthought.
2. **Film and Say Why become the differentiator.** Not buried, but framed as
   what makes this evaluation different from a spreadsheet shop. The Founder
   Story tier stays as the on-ramp for individuals.
3. **Free tools stay top-of-funnel.** Alchemy and the Diagnostic still draw
   people in. The Diagnostic in particular is a natural lead magnet for the
   evaluation buyer.

## The cost of the inversion

- The brand docs and the new hero voice were built film-first. An
  evaluation-forward hub partly contradicts `brand-context.md` ("This is Isaac's
  practice. Personal, singular, relational"). Agencies buy from practices that
  read as capable and a little institutional, which pulls against the intimate
  register.
- The two design systems problem gets sharper. Evaluation buyers expect the
  sober Brand A (Ink/Ochre/Teal) more than the Digital Liver palette.
- It risks burying the thing that is actually distinctive. If the hub reads like
  every other evaluation consultancy, the film and the methodology stop pulling.

## A third option

Lead with evaluation for the agency/association buyer, keep film-forward for the
individual buyer, and split them by entry path rather than by domain. One hub,
two front doors: `/evaluation` reads institutional, `/story` reads relational,
and the person is the through-line on both. This keeps the warm money lane
honest without flattening the practice into a vendor.

## What I need from Isaac

One call, not three. Pick the frame the hub leads with: film-forward,
evaluation-forward, or the two-front-doors split. The consolidation schedule and
the homepage copy both branch from that single choice.
