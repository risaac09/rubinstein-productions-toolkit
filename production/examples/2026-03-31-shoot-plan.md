> An example from a past shoot, kept as a model for a short plan. The standard for every shoot is [capture-standard](../capture-standard.md) (`production/capture-standard.md`). This plan predates the standard and shoots 30 fps; follow the standard's table.

# Shoot Plan: Tuesday March 31
*Two short pieces. One hour total. iPhone is fine.*

---

## SHOOT 1: Camera Packing (30 min)

A hands-and-objects sequence. No talking.

### Setup
- Natural window light. Table or desk surface.
- Camera body and lens laid out. A box or padded case. A shipping label if you have one.
- iPhone filming from above, or from across the table at a slight angle.
- No ring light. No tripod needed; prop the phone against something.

### Shot list
1. **Hands on the camera.** Pick it up. Turn it over. Hold the lens. 10 seconds.
2. **Wrapping.** Wrap the camera in a cloth or padding. Place it in the box. 15 seconds.
3. **The lens.** Hold it, place it in. 10 seconds.
4. **Closing the box.** Tape it or close the lid. 5 seconds.
5. **The label.** Write on it or place it on the box. If you have no label, skip this.
6. **Final shot.** The sealed box on the table. Hold for 5 seconds.

### Technical notes
- Film in 4K if the iPhone supports it (Settings → Camera → Record Video → 4K 30fps).
- Shoot each action as a separate clip. Easier to edit.
- Silence or ambient room sound. No music.
- Total raw footage: about 2-3 minutes.

---

## SHOOT 2: Talking-head clip (30 min)

### Setup
- iPhone propped at eye level (stack books, use a shelf).
- Natural light. Face a window. No overhead lighting.
- Quiet room. No echo if possible.
- Frame: head and shoulders, some space above. Slightly off-center is fine.
- Neutral clothing. No logos.

### Technical notes
- Talk from notes, not a script.
- 60-90 seconds. If it runs longer, trim later.
- Do 3 takes. The second or third is usually the best.
- Minor jump cuts are fine in this format.
- Look at the camera lens, not the screen.
- Start talking right away. End clean and stop.

---

## AFTER FILMING

1. AirDrop or transfer all clips to your laptop.
2. Put the raw footage in one folder named for the shoot date.
3. Camera packing: trim the clips in iMovie or DaVinci Resolve and export.
4. Talking head: pick the best take, trim dead air from the start and end, export.

---

## TIME BUDGET

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Set up table and light for camera packing | 5 min |
| Film camera packing clips | 10 min |
| Set up for the talking-head clip | 5 min |
| Film 3 takes | 10 min |
| Review and select takes | 10 min |
| Basic editing (trim and export) | 20 min |
| **Total** | **~60 min** |
