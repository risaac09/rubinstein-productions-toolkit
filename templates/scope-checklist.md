# Scope Checklist
## Every field other documents assign to the signed scope

*One page. Fill it in from the [Client Onboarding template](client-onboarding.md) before the proposal goes out. Each line names the field and the document that assigns it. The checklist holds no prices and no contract wording.*

---

## Consent

- [ ] **Consent clause.** The clause that proves tier 1. With no clause, tier 1 has no evidence. Assigned by [Consent Evidence](../production/consent-evidence.md#the-one-artifact-per-tier).
- [ ] **Consent tier offered, per participant.** Recorded in the onboarding template's consent tier step. Assigned by [Consent Evidence](../production/consent-evidence.md).

## Capture

- [ ] **Frame rate.** 23.976 unless the scope says 29.97, and then 29.97 on every camera of the project. Assigned by [Capture Standard](../production/capture-standard.md#frame-rate-and-shutter).
- [ ] **Vertical or square cut planned: yes or no.** Yes means the GH7 shoots Open Gate. No means UHD 16:9. Assigned by [Capture Standard](../production/capture-standard.md#resolution-and-framing).

## Approval

- [ ] **Approver, by name and role.** The person on camera, and the commissioner too where they are a different person. Assigned by [the final-approval gate](../production/resolve-template-spec.md#the-final-approval-gate).
- [ ] **Round limit R.** The number of change rounds before a further round needs the commissioner's written agreement. Assigned by [the final-approval gate](../production/resolve-template-spec.md#the-final-approval-gate).

## Disclosure

- [ ] **Machine-assisted steps that will be used.** The scope names them, and the client sees the list before work starts and again at handover. Assigned by [Credits and AI Disclosure](../production/credits-and-disclosure.md).
- [ ] **Steps the client opts out of.** A client can ask for a step to be left out. The answer is yes unless it makes the work impossible, and then the scope is renegotiated. Assigned by [Credits and AI Disclosure](../production/credits-and-disclosure.md).

## Return

- [ ] **Drive size limit.** The size above which a drive ships in place of a download link. Assigned by [Return Package](../production/return-package.md#medium).
