# For Writers

You write narrative, copy, or story development, and you want to see how a working practice encodes voice, mines sessions for language, and keeps its published work honest. This path covers the voice system, the excavation tools, and the editorial disciplines.

## First hour

1. **[Brand Context](../../prompts/brand-context.md)**. The voice bible: positioning, audience personas, tone adjectives, and hard creative constraints. The constraints are the interesting part. Never frame the client as broken. No savior framing. No fabricated case studies. Steal the structure and swap in your own rules.
2. **[Glossary](../../methodology/glossary.md)**. The vocabulary the rest of the corpus assumes, with the academic sources named.
3. **[Content Strategy](../../CONTENT-STRATEGY.md)**. The publishing playbook: two audiences, four content pillars, a launch sequence, and explicit rules for what never gets published. "Publish the methodology, not the diary" is the sentence to keep.

## Where the language comes from

The practice treats facilitated sessions as the raw material for all written work. Two documents show how language gets surfaced and captured.

- **[Session Facilitation Guide](../../methodology/session-facilitation-guide.md)**. Read the core question bank and the section on capturing the gold. The questions are designed to produce sentences people did not know they had. That is the writer's supply chain.
- **[Nomadic Indicators Codebook](../../methodology/nomadic-indicators-codebook.md)**. Written for evaluators, useful to writers. The codes mark the moments in a transcript where new metaphors emerge and a person's account of themselves shifts. Coded transcripts are pre-sorted ore.

## Editorial disciplines

Three practices keep the published work credible over time.

- **[Source Tracking Protocol](../../methodology/source-tracking-protocol.md)**. Five provenance categories for every claim, and a standing rule against invented or vague-authority citations. If you publish research-adjacent writing, adopt this whole.
- **[Negative Archive Ritual](../../methodology/negative-archive-ritual.md)**. A `scraps/` folder for what a project refused, cut, or withheld, kept visible as a record of editorial judgment. The cut is part of the work.
- **[Seed Bed](../../seed-bed/SKILL.md)**. An intake protocol for ideas that are not ready to be classified. Capture raw, review weekly, let the idea reveal its type. Useful for anyone whose drafts folder is a graveyard.

## First working session

Pick one piece you are currently writing. Run it against the Brand Context constraints as if they were your own, then log what you cut into a scraps file per the Negative Archive Ritual. One pass through both tools teaches more than a full read of either.

## What to adapt rather than adopt

The voice adjectives, personas, and taglines are specific to one practice and one person. The transferable layer is structural: a constraints list enforced on every draft, a question bank that generates client language, a provenance rule for claims, and a visible record of cuts.
