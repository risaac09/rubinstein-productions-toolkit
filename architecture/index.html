The Orchestrator

Say the word and the spine wakes. Prompt to ship, with the judgment in the middle and the diversity on the outside.

Trigger agent agents activate all agents
Spine — linear, three passes
1
Prompt
capture raw
›

Echo the request back in your own words. Don't clean it, don't solve it, don't classify it yet. Typos mean velocity. Name the one or two real unknowns instead of guessing them away.

Hands off: the request, stated plainly, plus the real unknowns.
2
Orchestrator
first pass · read the field
›

Decompose into workstreams. Estimate blast radius. Draft a candidate routing table from the existing skills. This pass frames the work. It does not deploy anything yet.

Hands off: workstreams, blast radius, a candidate routing table.
3
Strategic Coordinator
the digital twin · second pass
›

The neck of the balloon. Holds the strategic sense of where things are and what's useful now. Grounds on stack-data, applies the COO guardrails, then commits: these agents deploy, in this shape, for these reasons. Flags every contested decision. The only layer allowed to say none of this is worth doing right now.

Hands off: a committed deployment plan, with reasons, contested calls flagged.
Balloon — jump out, then come back
Divergence
4
Jump out
parallel specialist agents
›

Deploy the committed agents in parallel. Each runs isolated on its workstream with a tight brief. They don't coordinate mid-flight. Isolation is what keeps their outputs genuinely different.

✦
Mini-arenas
optional · recommended by default
›

For each contested decision, seat two or three agents on opposing sides of the real disagreement. One exchange minimum: state the case, answer the strongest objection. Conflict as a generative force.

The output is a spread of distinct, defensible options with each cost named. Not a blended compromise. Skip an arena only when a decision is genuinely uncontested.

The Arena, from seed-bed (Claim → Arena), scaled down to a single decision inside a single run.
Convergence
5
Come back together
twin reels it back in
›

The digital twin merges the specialist outputs and resolves each arena's spread into one ranked proposal: recommended first, the live alternatives kept visible, the cost of each named. Tensions that didn't resolve get surfaced, not buried.

Hands off: one coherent proposal, ranked, alternatives still showing.
Gate
6
Quality Assurance
at least one · adversarial
›

Nothing ships before the gate. Check the proposal against voice (no em-dashes, no rule-of-three, no promotional verbs, active voice), grounding (every claim traces to a source), constraints (capacity, price floors, blast radius), and fit (does it answer the Pass 1 prompt, or did it drift).

On fail: loop back to divergence with the failure named. Don't patch around it.
