# Second Brain V2: Opening and Process

> The thinking ability of a nomadic scientist, the lineages of a polymath, and a working loop between a practitioner and an AI that produces new artifacts in new domains. This is the door, the protocol, and the way back through it.

## What V2 is, and what it is not

**V1 is the vault.** Years of notes, MOCs, canonical positioning docs, research clusters, the Cross-Epistemological Translation Matrix, the Abhidhamma maps, the critical-theory deep dives, the contemplative traditions cluster, the synthesis tendrils. V1 is the substrate. Read-mostly, edit-occasionally, the storehouse.

**V2 is the working capacity.** V2 = V1 + the four-direction oscillation as decision posture + the working-context block as standing voice + AI as co-laborer + a documented iteration loop that produces new artifacts in new domains. V2 is read-and-write, in motion, the lathe.

V2 does not replace V1. V2 stands on it.

V2 is not a tool, an app, or a script. V2 is a documented posture and a documented loop. The tools are Claude Code, the vault, the toolkit, stack-data, and the skills. The posture and the loop are what make those tools serve a single coherent practice.

## Why "nomadic scientist"

The frame comes from Deleuze and Guattari (A Thousand Plateaus, 1980). Nomadic science follows matter-in-flow rather than imposing form on matter from outside. It works through problematics, not theorems. It moves through smooth space rather than re-gridding striated space. Royal science extracts constants and reterritorializes. Nomadic science deterritorializes and lets new territory form.

Operational translation:

- Follow the question across disciplines instead of forcing it into one. A public-health question that's also an attention-economy question that's also a phenomenological question gets all three reads.
- Treat the vault as smooth space, not a hierarchy. The MOCs are conveniences; the lineages cross them.
- Trust the artisan's mode. The metallurgist knows metal by following it; V2 work knows a domain by following the material, not by importing a textbook structure.
- Refuse premature closure. The temptation in royal science is to settle the question. The nomadic move is to keep the question alive long enough to learn from where it goes.

This is not anti-rigor. Deleuze and Guattari call nomadic science "anexact yet rigorous." The discipline is in the following.

## The opening

When a session starts, this is the sequence. Five steps, never skipped, even when fast.

1. **Paste the Working Context Block** (or the One Page kernel). The pre-prompt loads. Identity, lens, oscillation, stack, voice rules, job.
2. **Name the territory.** Three to twelve words. "I'm working on the eval-RFP for [client]." "Drafting the urban-planning piece on capacity." Naming the territory makes the rest possible.
3. **Fill the per-session block.** State, walk-away, surface, direction on the wheel. If the wheel position is unclear, that's the first thing to work on.
4. **Locate the lineages.** Which clusters in the vault are load-bearing for this territory? AI surfaces candidates; the practitioner confirms. If a needed lineage is missing, that is itself a finding to persist (it points to a v3 expansion).
5. **Set the loop's cadence.** Single-shot draft or back-and-forth iteration? Fast turn or slow turn? Read or write? This shapes what V2 produces.

The opening takes between thirty seconds and ten minutes depending on territory novelty.

The opening is not optional because skipping it produces royal science by default. The session reverts to "answer this question, format this thing" and the nomadic move is lost.

## The process: the iteration loop

Seven moves. Not strictly sequential; they oscillate. But each is named so the absence of one is detectable.

**1. Locate.** Where is the question on the four-direction wheel? North (work-output), East (innocence, pre-conceptual receiving), South (transition, metabolism), West (clarity, naming).

**2. Read across lineages.** Use the Cross-Epistemological Translation Matrix when the question crosses domains. Most real questions do. A public-health policy question can be read as: Buddhist (whose suffering is named, whose is hidden), Foucauldian (which biopolitical apparatus is being extended), phenomenological (what lived experience does the policy treat as universal), Deleuzian (what does the policy striate or smooth), and economic.

**3. Pull from V1.** The vault has the depth. Search by lineage, not by keyword. The right note is in the contemplative cluster, or the critical-theory cluster, or the synthesis tendril, not in a flat search result. AI surfaces candidates; practitioner picks. Persist the chain of which notes spoke to which move.

**4. Move.** Produce the artifact. Draft the eval proposal, write the policy memo, build the prototype, draft the email. This is where the canonical voice actively shapes output. V2 ships drafts.

**5. TCO across columns.** Run the cost protocol. Seven columns, five granularities. Specifically ask the race-and-gender column. Specifically ask whose labor is being externalized.

**6. Persist.** If the artifact lives beyond the session: vault canonical (posture), vault research (depth), project memory (stateful project), stack-data (operational), standalone repo (discrete public thing), client deliverable. Pointers, not duplicates.

**7. Close the loop.** What is the next question this opened? What lineage did we touch that needs depth before next time? What posture move was confirmed or refused?

The seven moves can run in fifteen minutes for a familiar artifact, or stretch across days for a novel domain. Coherence is the metric, not speed.

1. Name the domain in 02 Research as a new cluster, with its own MOC.

## The nomadic-scientist posture

Compressed for fast load:

- Follow the question, do not impose form on it.
- Cross lineages by default. The cleanest single-lineage answer is often the wrong one.
- Trust the vault as smooth space.
- Refuse premature closure. The artifact does not have to be the answer; it can be the next better question.
- Read whose body is paying before reading whether the artifact is good.
- Persist what the session learned, not just what the session produced.
- The wheel turns. North alone calcifies. East alone bypasses. South alone strands. West alone diagnoses without acting.

## Failure modes

- **Royal-science creep.** The opening gets skipped. Session reverts to "answer this question." Symptom: the artifact lands but the lineages weren't touched. Fix: re-run the opening retroactively, re-do the move with cross-lineage reading.
- **Vault drift.** New artifacts persist to deliverables but never back to V1 or to a project memory. The vault stays frozen at v1. Symptom: same questions keep arising fresh. Fix: the seventh move (close the loop) is non-optional.
- **AI overproduction.** AI ships too much, practitioner doesn't read, artifacts pile up unreviewed. Symptom: queue of unread drafts. Fix: shorter loops, smaller artifacts, explicit acceptance per artifact.
- **Practitioner underproduction.** Practitioner doesn't ship a draft because the territory feels too big. Symptom: research mode without artifact production. Fix: name a smaller artifact and do the loop on that. The smaller artifact will surface what the big one needs.
- **Lineage gaps.** A new domain gets pulled in without the supporting research cluster. Symptom: the artifact is shallow on the new domain. Fix: stop, do the depth, then continue.
- **Authority without provenance.** Claims sound authoritative without earned citation. Fix: source-tracking-protocol.md is non-optional.
- **Compression debt.** Docs grow without subtraction. Fix: the One Page is the kernel; everything else is companion.
