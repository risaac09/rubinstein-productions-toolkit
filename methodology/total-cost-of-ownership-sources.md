# Total Cost of Ownership, Sources

Companion source document for Total Cost of Ownership. Lists every named theorist, tradition, or specific concept cited in the main doc, with provenance category and citation in APA 7th edition.

Provenance categories follow Source-Tracking Protocol:

- **VERIFIED**, verified in this session against the source
- **CITED**, cited from training data, well-attested in standard literature, not directly verified this session
- **QUEUED**, cited from training data, requires verification before any external use
- **PERSONAL**, Isaac's own work, vault, or stated experience

---

## Carl Gustav Jung

**Concept cited:** thinking function (North); feeling function (South)
**Status:** CITED

Jung, C. G. (1971). *Psychological types* (R. F. C. Hull, Trans.). Princeton University Press. (Original work published 1921)

*Source of the four-function typology (thinking, feeling, sensation, intuition). The thinking/feeling distinction used in the main doc for North and South maps to Jung's rational functions. Verify specific passages on the ordering function (thinking) and valuation function (feeling) before external use.*

## W. E. B. Du Bois

**Concept cited:** double consciousness
**Status:** CITED

Du Bois, W. E. B. (2007). *The souls of black folk*. Oxford University Press. (Original work published 1903)

*Introduces "double consciousness" in Chapter 1, "Of Our Spiritual Strivings": "this sense of always looking at one's self through the eyes of others." The metabolic-cost reading in the main doc extends Du Bois's psychological framing into a contemporary stress-physiology register.*

## Frantz Fanon

**Concept cited:** epidermalization
**Status:** CITED

Fanon, F. (1967). *Black skin, white masks* (C. L. Markmann, Trans.). Grove Press. (Original work published 1952)

*Chapter 5, "The Fact of Blackness," develops the concept of epidermalization: the inferiority complex inscribed onto the body and the skin. Specific page references queued for verification.*

## Maurice Merleau-Ponty

**Concept cited:** the lived body
**Status:** CITED

Merleau-Ponty, M. (2012). *Phenomenology of perception* (D. A. Landes, Trans.). Routledge. (Original work published 1945)

*Develops the lived body (corps vécu) as distinct from the objective body, foundational to embodied phenomenology. The "hand that touches before the mind names" image used in the main doc tracks Merleau-Ponty's chiasm of touching and being touched, developed more fully in The Visible and the Invisible (1968, posthumous).*

## Edmund Husserl

**Concept cited:** Lebenswelt (lifeworld)
**Status:** CITED

Husserl, E. (1970). *The crisis of European sciences and transcendental phenomenology: An introduction to phenomenological philosophy* (D. Carr, Trans.). Northwestern University Press. (Original work published 1936)

*Lebenswelt is developed in Crisis as the pre-scientific ground of all theoretical activity: the field of immediate experience that science abstracts from and forgets. Central to the East-direction "innocence" reading. Specific section references queued.*

## Judith Butler

**Concept cited:** performativity (of gender)
**Status:** CITED

Butler, J. (1990). *Gender trouble: Feminism and the subversion of identity*. Routledge.

*Develops performativity: gender is not what one is but what one repeatedly does, congealed through ritualized repetition into the appearance of natural being. Specific page references for the "learned before knowing one is learning" reading queued.*

**Status:** QUEUED

*The phrase "administered world" (verwaltete Welt) recurs across Adorno's late work. Specific passage in Minima Moralia needs verification. Also developed in:*

## Herbert Marcuse

**Concept cited:** one-dimensional thought
**Status:** CITED

Marcuse, H. (1991). *One-dimensional man: Studies in the ideology of advanced industrial society* (2nd ed.). Beacon Press. (Original work published 1964)

*Titular concept: the collapse of dialectical (two-dimensional) thinking into single-dimensional affirmative thought under technological rationality. Chapter 1, "The New Forms of Control."*

## Martin Heidegger

**Concept cited:** Lichtung (the clearing)
**Status:** CITED

Heidegger, M. (1962). *Being and time* (J. Macquarrie & E. Robinson, Trans.). Harper & Row. (Original work published 1927)

*Lichtung, the clearing, appears across Heidegger's work. In Being and Time as the opening (Da) in which beings can show themselves. Developed further in:*

Heidegger, M. (1972). The end of philosophy and the task of thinking. In *On time and being* (J. Stambaugh, Trans., pp. 55–73). Harper & Row. (Original lecture 1964)

## Sara Ahmed

**Concept cited:** affective economies; how certain bodies are positioned as the ones who feel
**Status:** VERIFIED

Ahmed, S. (2004). Affective economies. *Social Text*, *22*(2), 117–139. https://doi.org/10.1215/01642472-22-2_79-117

*Develops affective economies: emotions circulate as forms of capital, sticking to certain bodies and not others. The "positioned as the ones who feel while others are positioned as the ones who know" reading in the main doc draws on this and on the book-length development.* Verified 2026-05-23 via [Duke University Press](https://read.dukeupress.edu/social-text/article/22/2%20(79)/117/32675/Affective-Economies).

**Book-length development:**
Ahmed, S. (2014). *The cultural politics of emotion* (2nd ed.). Edinburgh University Press. (Original work published 2004)

## Kimberlé W. Crenshaw

**Status:** VERIFIED

## Stephen W. Porges

**Concept cited:** polyvagal state; vagal tone; dorsal vagal shutdown
**Status:** CITED

Porges, S. W. (2011). *The polyvagal theory: Neurophysiological foundations of emotions, attachment, communication, and self-regulation*. W. W. Norton.

*Book-length statement of polyvagal theory. Original framing in:*

Porges, S. W. (1995). Orienting in a defensive world: Mammalian modifications of our evolutionary heritage. A polyvagal theory. *Psychophysiology*, *32*(4), 301–318.

**Note on contemporary scientific status:** Polyvagal theory's specific neuroanatomical claims (myelinated vagal pathways unique to mammals, the precise role of the nucleus ambiguus in social engagement) have been contested in recent peer review. See for example:

Grossman, P., & Taylor, E. W. (2007). Toward understanding respiratory sinus arrhythmia: Relations to cardiac vagal tone, evolution and biobehavioral functions. *Biological Psychology*, *74*(2), 263–285.

*The phenomenological account of co-regulation and autonomic state remains widely used in trauma and somatic therapy. Use with awareness of the live empirical debate.*

## Buddhist Abhidhamma

**Concepts cited:** cetanā (volition); phassa (contact); vedanā (feeling-tone); the 52 cetasikas (mental factors); paññā (wisdom); the three marks of existence (anicca, dukkha, anattā); the unwholesome roots (greed, hatred, delusion); the beautiful cetasikas (faith, mindfulness, moral shame, equanimity); brahmaviharas and their near-enemies.

**Status:** CITED (synthesis-not-translation; see verification queue)

**Primary literature (translated):**

Anuruddha. (1993). *A comprehensive manual of Abhidhamma: The Abhidhammattha Sangaha* (B. Bodhi, Ed. & Trans., with U Rewata Dhamma & U Sīlānanda). Buddhist Publication Society.

*Standard primer for the 52 cetasikas and the Abhidhamma system. Bhikkhu Bodhi's edition is the authoritative English-language entry point.*

Buddhaghosa. (1991). *The path of purification: Visuddhimagga* (B. Ñāṇamoli, Trans., 5th ed.). Buddhist Publication Society. (Original work 5th c. CE)

*Source for the brahmaviharas with their near-enemies framework; comprehensive exposition of the cetasikas and the Abhidhamma method.*

**Conditional relations:**

The 24 paccayas of the Patthana, the seventh book of the Abhidhamma Pitaka, ground the conditioning analysis used in companion docs.

**Secondary / applied:**

van Gorkom, N. (2010). *Abhidhamma in daily life* (4th rev. ed.). Zolag.

*Practical and applied framing of Abhidhamma psychology, accessible register.*

## Critical Theory (Frankfurt School broadly), reification

**Concept cited:** reification; categories that are pre-given suppress lived experience
**Status:** CITED

Lukács, G. (1971). *History and class consciousness: Studies in Marxist dialectics* (R. Livingstone, Trans.). MIT Press. (Original work published 1923)

*Source for the modern concept of reification (Verdinglichung): the process by which the products of human activity appear as objective realities outside of human practice. The chapter "Reification and the Consciousness of the Proletariat" is the locus classicus.*

## Open Circle Foundation, source of the four-direction synthesis Isaac received

**Status:** PERSONAL / METHODOLOGICAL

The directional assignments (North = work, East = innocence, South = transition, West = clarity) used in the main doc came to Isaac through Open Circle Foundation programming. The framework as it appears in the canonical doc is Isaac's synthesis of that received teaching layered with subsequent reading in Jung, Husserl, the Mahasi tradition, and the named theorists above.

Open Circle's public-facing materials (as of 2026-05-23) do not include this directional framework. Their public methodology is described as "nature-based rites of passage" combining "ancient practices of initiation with contemporary science, eco-psychology, and developmental learning." The directional synthesis presented in the main doc is therefore Isaac's, not Open Circle's public teaching. Verified 2026-05-23 via [weareopencircle.org](https://www.weareopencircle.org/).

Isaac has stated a "productive distance" relationship with Open Circle going forward: continued credit for what was received, no endorsement.
