New audio-state command reads a timeline's audio from Resolve (tracks, Audio* item properties, audio mapping, queued jobs' audio fields, loudness-meter settings) and names what the API cannot read; deliver-check and the deliver_check tool take it with --audio-state / audio_state and compare the queued jobs' audio codec, sample rate and bit depth with the destination. queue_render now restores the Deliver page through a render guard (format, codec, render mode and the timeline's mark In/Out, read back and checked) and names AudioSampleRate and the render range as settings Resolve cannot read back.
