rpstills develop: the look's tint bound is per backend (`solve.tint_max: {apple: 50, lightcraft: 6}`), since Apple's CIRAWFilter and LightCraft use different tint units. The solve reads the active backend's bound and records it in calibration.json; a plain number still bounds every backend, and an unknown backend name fails the look. The trial look moves to apple 50 (from a shared 6 that pinned tint on every apple frame). LightCraft is described as the opt-in RAW backend behind RPSTILLS_LIGHTCRAFT, with Apple the default.
