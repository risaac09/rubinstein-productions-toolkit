vertcut cut prints an estimate of its wall time (clip seconds x RATIO, one third) and refuses a batch estimated over 30 minutes, or one that would replace an existing output, unless --reviewed names the gate.json of a gate-packet and --review-log names a review log holding a go line for that packet (matched by its packet sha256, read with tastedistill.read_events). Both files must sit outside any git tree; a refusal prints the gate-packet command to run.
