Shot lists compile into the draft farm's manifest: `resolve_workflow.py shotlist check` validates a footage-first list of shots in story order, and `shotlist compile` joins it with a cut plan (or a fixed period) into manifest.json with retime and reframe-pan entries, the plain method.json the farm reads beside it, and, for 9:16, a crops.json of static centres. An excluded take is refused as the draft farm refuses it.
