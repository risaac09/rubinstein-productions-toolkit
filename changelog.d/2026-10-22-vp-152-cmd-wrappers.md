resolve_workflow.py gains seven commands, one thin rpresolve/cmd_<name>.py each, for the offline modules that came in with #247: interchange, shots, wordsnap, beats, roomtone, dxmetrics and duckplan. Each wrapper parses with the module's own add_arguments and hands off to its run(), so the flags and the work stay in one place; the numpy ones still build the parser on the stdlib-only Python and say numpy is missing when run. The tests for those modules now skip without numpy or ffmpeg, as the stdlib-only 3.9 job that #228 points at every test module expects. Follows VP-152 (#228).
