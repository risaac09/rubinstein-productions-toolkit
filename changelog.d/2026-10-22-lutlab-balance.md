lutlab gains `balance`: per-shot exposure and white balance for video (a clip or a folder of frames), neutralised on a neutral reference with exposure set on the cheek and skin hue left alone (the house rule for stills), or solved onto a skin target from a plain JSON of targets; `--wb-from` carries one shot's white balance to a shot with no neutral. It writes a row per shot, an ASC CDL (.cc) and baked .cube in the camera's code values, and a set-consistency table. Shared CDL helpers live in `lutlab.cdl`.
