production/rpresolve: besttake proposes an order for the takes of one answer from a folder or manifest (speech share, pauses, words per minute, loudness, noise floor, SNR, clipping, and for video in-camera cuts and the Vision face share), as JSON and a Markdown table with a reason line that only quotes the numbers. It never names a best take; the ranking formula is one documented table, changeable with `--formula`.
