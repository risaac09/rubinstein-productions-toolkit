reframe_plan with items takes a hand file (hand): a crop centre as a share of the source width, and optionally height and a zoom (a multiplier on the fill scale, 1 to 4), for each item with no usable face, so framing goes through reframe_plan and reframe_apply even for a shot with no face. A hand item has status hand, source hand and no head check, and reframe_apply treats it like a pass; an item with a face keeps its face crop and gets a review line with the difference. Bad shares, zooms, duplicate or missing item numbers, and hand without items are refused before any work. Not run against a real Resolve. A hand entry that cannot be used says so in the item's review list.
