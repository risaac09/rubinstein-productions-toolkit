Transcripts are screened before anything reads them. vertcut transcribe keeps the raw Whisper JSON as <name>.raw.json (mode 600, read by nothing by default), screens it with tools/vertcut/lib/segkind.py while the audio is still in the temp folder, and writes the safe <name>.json, where a segment screened as sung, music or a hallucination keeps its timestamps and gets a [singing], [music] or [inaudible] label in place of its text, plus <name>.kinds.json with kinds and time ranges only. segkind decides from numbers, never from the words: Whisper's confidence and compression figures, words per second, repetition, and pitch from the audio. Every transcript reader in rpresolve and vertcut goes through rpresolve.guard (cutlist.load_words), which screens a raw JSON in memory and refuses a .raw.json by name; selects proposes no window across a withheld span, endcheck fails a span over one, trim_review reports it as a non-speech row, vertcut cut refuses a clip over singing or music unless --allow-music and draws the label as a cue, and create_captions warns where a kinds sidecar places sung audio. New vertcut kinds prints kinds, times and counts only; a sung.tsv is authoritative in either direction. production/tests/test_segkind.py covers the classifier on synthetic audio, the guard, the wiring, and scans the sources for a reader that skips the guard.
