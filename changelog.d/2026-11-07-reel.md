`rpresolve.reel` (`resolve_workflow reel`): a talking-head cut manifest and a music track become a reel plan with every cut on a beat and outside every word, the hook ending on a downbeat, dissolves where the pauses hold them, alternating punch-ins, cutout marks and the music bed; `preview` renders the plan as a 1080x1920 H.264 file through ffmpeg on the same frames the FCPXML carries (reel.md).
