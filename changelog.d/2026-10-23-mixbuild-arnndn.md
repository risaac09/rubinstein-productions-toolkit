mixbuild: arnndn (RNNoise) is a working denoise method. Its .rnnn model comes from the step's `model` or `RPRESOLVE_RNNOISE_MODEL`; a missing, relative or unreadable model refuses the spec instead of skipping the denoise, the path is escaped for ffmpeg's filter syntax, its 10 ms delay is measured and trimmed like the other denoisers', and render.json records each model's sha256. The lav and boom presets now denoise with arnndn (picked over afftdn in a blind A/B on speech); a bare `denoise` and every other preset keep afftdn or none, since RNNoise is trained on speech. The toolkit ships no model; the tests generate a tiny zero-weight one.
