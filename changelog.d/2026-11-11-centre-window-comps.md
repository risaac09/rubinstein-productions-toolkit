Centre the scale-window and crop-window comps on the 9:16 crop centre in vertical_apply, instead of leaving them as unmoved_comps. The cause of trap T34 is the item's Pan shifting the comp's output: a crop-window gets Rectangle1.Center on the crop centre, and a scale-window gets Transform1.Pivot there and nothing else (Pivot only anchors the scaling, so the crop-centre content stays put through the window and the snap to full frame). Both are read back, a centre of 0.5 changes nothing, and unmoved_comps no longer names these two kinds. The fix is from Fusion's documented Transform behaviour and is not yet confirmed on a render; the scale-window's size at 9:16 stays untested and open.
