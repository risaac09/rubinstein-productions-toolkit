`assemble_timeline` sets a pool clip's Pixel Aspect Ratio (the `PAR` property, nothing else) to the squeeze a person recorded for its card, with read-back, before items are placed; a clip holding another non-square value is reported and left alone and a BRAW file is never written. The dry run also says when the timeline's shape would crop more than 5 percent of the desqueezed picture and names the shape that fits, without choosing one. `deliver_check` takes the manifest and adds a shape row and, for anamorphic sources, a squeeze row that fails a file rendered at the squeezed sensor aspect. The Resolve MCP's additive-only rule now names this as its sixth exception.
