`rpresolve.interchange` writes the reel fields into FCPXML: a per-span `transition` (a Cross Dissolve centred on the cut, refused without source handles), a static `scale` (an adjust-transform Resolve reads as Zoom), a `layer` (a clip with alpha on the lane above the span) and a manifest-level `music` bed on the lane below; EDL and OTIO warn that they drop them. The vocabulary follows what Resolve 21.2 imports (resolve-mcp.md, FCPXML import).
