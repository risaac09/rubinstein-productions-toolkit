Adds rpresolve.hooklint, one shared hook-text lint (module and python3 -m CLI): nine words or fewer, two lines of at most 16 characters on 9:16 and 24 elsewhere, hold of at least max(0.8 s, 0.3 s per word), starts at frame 0, no em-dash, no emoji, and a banned-phrase row that reports SKIP unless a list is supplied. It checks supplied lines and never writes or suggests copy.
