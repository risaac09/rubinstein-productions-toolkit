`vertcut manifest SRC clips.tsv --words SRC.json -o OUT.json` turns a cuts worksheet into a version 2 cut manifest that `endcheck`, `interchange` and `reframe_plan` read, so a vertcut worksheet can take the Resolve route. It reads rows with vertcut's own worksheet parser, reads `HH:MM:SS:FF` the NTSC-correct way `cutlist` does (so a 29.97 or 23.976 row lands on the same frame in both; `vertcut cut` still adds `FF / fps` to whole seconds, so the two can differ there), applies `--offset`, warns when an in or out lands inside a spoken word, merges rows that share an id into one clip, and refuses an output path inside any repository.
