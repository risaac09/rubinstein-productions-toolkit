scene-colour: the colour axis of the per-shot scene model. One block per perceive packet (near-neutral cast, skin hue and Lab, an 8-colour Lab palette, the shadow-to-highlight tint, detect's input colour space), each null number with a reason, the taste fields marked, harmony left as a review question, and each shot's CIEDE2000 palette distance and hue arc from the one before. Documented in production/scene-colour.md.
