vertcut cut gains `--review` with 2 to 4 explicit `--variant` specs (crop x, caption style, hook on or off) for one worksheet row, rendered into a lettered, loudness-matched reel-ab packet with a sealed key that review-log reads. `--ledger-row PATH` writes a release-ledger row stub per clip through delivercheck, a destination with a loudness target sets the default `--lufs` and says so on stderr, a 59.94 source is written at 30000/1001 instead of 30, and `--no-hook` drops a hook. The README covers these and the batch review gate.
