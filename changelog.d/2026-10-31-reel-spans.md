resolve_workflow.py reel-spans turns the start and end phrases an editor quotes into a checked vertcut cuts worksheet: in at the first word of the start phrase, out at the last word of the end phrase, with a per-row FAIL for a missing, repeated or reversed phrase and a WARN for a span over 90 s, under 5 s or ending mid-sentence. It proposes no spans and refuses any git tree. (RL-6)
