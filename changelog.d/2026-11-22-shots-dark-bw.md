decon recipe and compare find cuts in dark and black-and-white footage: with shots.build(adaptive=True) the scdet threshold scales down with the luma spread of frames that have a picture (never below 0.4 of the set value) and PySceneDetect runs luma-only on black and white; cuts in the first two frames are dropped. shots, qc and besttake keep the fixed threshold, and an explicit --threshold is never scaled. The decon compare JSON records each side's detector, threshold_used and low_contrast. Dissolves and dips on dark footage still use fixed grey limits, so cut recall with dissolves on is only partly better.
