A card record's `shoot` reads capture times and cameras from every original media file, stills included, so a stills-only card gets its real days, and `card set --refresh-shoot` never replaces a real shoot with file times or with nothing: it keeps the old one and the dry run says so. A root walk (`card check`, `card_check`) skips NAS and OS trash folders, where replaced sidecars wait and would count as a second card.
