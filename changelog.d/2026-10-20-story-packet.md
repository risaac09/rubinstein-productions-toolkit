Add the story-packet command to resolve_workflow.py: one Markdown file that holds a shoot as story material for the selects board. Per clip in capture order it gives the shoot-table row, an audio energy sparkline with the loud spans and the biggest rise, the words as timed phrases (Whisper loops marked), any picture labels from JSONL and, with --strips, a contact strip of eight frames (two rows of four) linked from each card. The clip id is the card folder (<CAM>-<id6>) and the file stem; clips sharing an id or a stem get nothing attached by it. It scores and chooses nothing. (VP-130)
